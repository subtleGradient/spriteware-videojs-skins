Keyboard & browser notes

Tab moves between controls. Enter or Space activates buttons. Timeline arrows seek five seconds; Home and End jump to either end. Volume uses arrow keys. Escape closes chapter selection or exits fullscreen.

Play and Pause are separate transport buttons, just like the original concept. iPhone may use its native fullscreen player and hardware volume. No autoplay, WebGL, canvas effects or duplicate mobile toolbar.
