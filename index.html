Keyboard & browser notes

Tab moves between controls. Enter or Space activates buttons. Timeline arrows seek five seconds; Home and End jump to either end. Volume uses arrow keys. Chapter selection supports Up/Down and Home/End. Escape closes it or exits video fullscreen. K toggles playback, M mutes, and F opens video fullscreen when a player control has focus.

Play and Pause are separate transport buttons, just like the original concept. Fullscreen shows only the video with native controls, including on iPhone. Device hardware may control volume. No autoplay, WebGL, canvas effects or duplicate mobile toolbar.
